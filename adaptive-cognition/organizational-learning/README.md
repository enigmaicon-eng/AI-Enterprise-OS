## Purpose

The organizational learning subsystem accumulates knowledge at the enterprise level — above individual agents and specific projects. It identifies recurring failures, preserves strategic lessons, and forms the institutional knowledge that makes the OS progressively more effective across all future work.

## Scope Hierarchy

Organizational learning operates at three scopes:
  PROJECT: specific to one project; least generalizable
  PORTFOLIO: generalizes across related projects
  ENTERPRISE: applies to all work the OS performs

Only PORTFOLIO and ENTERPRISE scope learning records are candidates for
promotion to the knowledge base (KU entries).

## Additive Constraint

Per INV-AC-07: organizational learning is always additive.
New records are created; existing validated records are never overwritten.
