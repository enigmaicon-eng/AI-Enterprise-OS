# Reasoning History
**ID:** AC-RH-000 | **Subsystem:** reasoning-history/ | **Version:** 1.0.0
**Updated:** 2026-05-17

---

## Purpose

Preserves the longitudinal reasoning record of the OS — decision chains, rationale, architectural choices, and strategic reasoning over time. Reasoning history enables the OS to build on its own prior reasoning, avoid repeating settled questions, and maintain coherent long-horizon decision-making.

## Key Principle

Reasoning history is DESCRIPTIVE, not PRESCRIPTIVE. It tells the OS what it has decided and why. It does not compel any future decision. Past reasoning is context; it is not a constraint unless explicitly promoted to a constraint through the governance process.
